# LK Brothers Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

LK Brothers Aircon provides aircon servicing, repairs, installation, and recurring maintenance for homes and businesses across Singapore. The redesigned homepage helps visitors compare promotions, choose a service based on their needs, and book with the workmanship warranty in view.

## Main Improvements

1. **Made promotions easier to compare.** The original rotating layout made it difficult to compare all three offers. Gold Servicing, Chemical Wash, and Yearly Servicing now appear together with unit counts, visit frequency, and GST-inclusive totals.
2. **Moved service guidance next to the service links.** The original layout placed the guidance away from the service choices. A short guide now helps customers choose where to start for routine maintenance, musty smells, leaks, or weak cooling.
3. **Clarified warranty coverage at booking.** The warranty scope was easy to miss before booking. The 90-day workmanship warranty now appears beside the booking buttons and specifies that it applies to eligible work.

## Design Improvements

The homepage follows a clear order from promotion comparison to service selection and booking. A consistent set of details makes the three offers easier to scan, while related guidance and warranty information sit beside the actions they support.
