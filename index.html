Before you book

What’s included and what to expect from your visit.

What’s included in routine aircon servicing?

It includes filter cleaning, surface cleaning of the evaporator coil, drainage vacuuming and a cooling performance check. The technician checks the unit before recommending deeper cleaning or repairs.

What should I share when I book?

Share your location, the number of units and any symptoms such as leaks or weak cooling. The team can help choose the service and arrange a suitable time.

Do you service aircon across Singapore?

Yes. LK Brothers serves HDB flats, condos, landed homes and commercial properties islandwide. Contact the team for a customised VRV or VRF quote.
